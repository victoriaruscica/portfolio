## Selected Projects

### Case Study 1 — Migrating Complexity Without Missing a Beat
*AppleCare BPR Systems Quality Manager, Apple (via RWS/Moravia)*

**The situation:** A major system migration needed to move across 12 cross-functional business units. With a team of 20+ stakeholders, 30+ SMEs, and engineers all needing to stay aligned, and customs regulatory compliance adding another layer, nobody could afford to get wrong. To make it more complex, both Apple and AppleCare workflows depended on this project landing on time, which meant close coordination with the Apple BPR team was non-negotiable.

**What I did:** I built the governance structure to hold it all together — mapping dependencies across every business unit, keeping regulatory requirements visible at every checkpoint, and making sure the right people were in the room before problems became blockers.

**The result:** 100% executive sign-off, delivered four weeks ahead of schedule.

---

### Case Study 2 — Fixing What Four Review Cycles Couldn't (With the Contract on the Line)
*Operations Project Manager, Gorilla Stationers*

**The situation:** A 1,000-line-item government compliance project had failed to hit its KPIs for four consecutive review cycles, and the stakes were about as high as they get: this contract was the company's main source of income. One more missed cycle, and it would have been annulled.

**What I did:** I ran root-cause analysis to find exactly where the process broke down, and traced a major piece of it to integration issues with a partner system that were causing incorrect pricing to display: a leading cause of the missed KPIs. From there, I redesigned the sourcing and buying workflows and built automated NetSuite reports that made the whole process faster and far more reliable.

**The result:** All KPIs hit on time and within scope for the first time in four review cycles, protecting the company's main revenue stream.
