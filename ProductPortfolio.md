## How I take a game to production

1. Define the product. Format, materials, components, and what the budget can carry.
2. Source and compare. Request quotes and samples, then compare offers, run sizes, and material availability, including overseas.
3. Develop art and translation. Brief artists, match them with authors, and manage deadlines and budgets.
4. Localize. Adapt and translate the game for each market.
5. Fulfill. Plan production, shipping, and delivery to the buyer.

## Localization

- Translating scientific papers and overseeing translations by other faculty and students at university.
