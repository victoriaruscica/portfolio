## 4. How I take a game to production
**Define the product.** Format, materials, components, and what the budget can carry.  
**Develop art and layout.** Brief artists, match them with authors, and manage deadlines and budgets.  
**Localize.** Adapt and translate the game for each market.  
**Source and compare.** Request quotes and samples, compare offers, run sizes, and material availability, including overseas.  
**Fulfill.** Plan production, shipping, and delivery to the buyer.

## 5. Localization
I've managed localization from three angles: overseeing 5+ localization projects at Apple through Moravia, including managing freelance and in-house translators; translating scientific papers and overseeing translations by other faculty and students at university; and translating my own games between Spanish, English, and Italian.
